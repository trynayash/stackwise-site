## Replies to expect, and what to say

**"How much?"** Websites from ₹34,999, SaaS products from ₹98,999, larger platforms quoted after a discovery call. Every project gets a written quote for its exact scope before work starts. (From the pricing page.)

**"Who else have you worked with?"** OM ENTERPRISE is the client project; the rest are the studio's own live products. Say that plainly. The case studies at stackwise.yashsuthar.dev/work cover the problem, the architecture and the trade-offs of each.

**"Do you work outside India?"** Yes, remotely, with calls inside the time-zone overlap and the same quote, NDA and code ownership. Prices in rupees, with approximate US dollar figures on the site.

**"Who made the film?"** We did, in code, with the site's own fonts, colours and the real screenshots. The source is on the `brand-film` branch of the site's repository.

# Sound design

Three layers: a music bed, spot effects, and the voice. The bed and effects are generated by `tools/make-audio.py` into `public/audio/` and mixed by the compositions (`src/Film.tsx`). The voice is recorded separately (see `VOICEOVER.md`) and mixed in with `--props='{"voiceover":true}'`.

## Mix targets

| Layer | File | Peak | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | `public/audio/voiceover.mp3` | −3 dBFS, about −16 LUFS integrated | Leads. Nothing else may mask 1–4 kHz while it speaks. |
| Effects | `public/audio/sfx.wav` | −5 dBFS | Punctuation only. Each cue is under 0.6 s except the riser. |
| Bed | `public/audio/bed.wav` | −9 dBFS, mixed at 0.9 | Low, warm, felt more than heard. Ducks under the voice by shape, not by compressor. |

As rendered without the voice, the bed and effects measure about −24 LUFS integrated with a −5 dBFS peak (ffmpeg `ebur128`), so the voice can sit on top at −16 LUFS without anything being pulled down. Final loudness: −14 LUFS integrated for Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn; −16 LUFS for a website autoplay version; true peak −1 dBTP. Do the final measure and limiter pass in DaVinci Resolve (Fairlight) after the voice is in.

## Music bed

A deliberately simple bed so the voice and the type carry the film:

- Two sines on A1 (55 Hz, one detuned +0.3 Hz for slow beating), A2 and E3, with 0.11 Hz vibrato, breathing on a 7-second cycle.
- Low-passed noise at −30 dB for air.
- A section curve that rises through the complexity scene, drops a step at the brand reveal (so the sub hit has room), sits lower under the showcase, warms slightly on the paper scene and resolves to silence by 43.3 s.
- A soft filtered click every half second through the process scene (27–34 s), 120 BPM, so the four steps feel like they are on a clock.

If a licensed track replaces it, match these shapes: minimal, no melody competing with the voice, one lift into 11 s, a steady quiet pulse at 27–34 s, and a clean ending at 43.3 s. Around 120 BPM suits the step timing (each step is 38 frames, about 1.27 s).

## Voice treatment

High-pass at 80 Hz, gentle de-ess, 2–3 dB of compression at a 3:1 ratio, no reverb. If the bed and voice fight at 100–200 Hz, cut the bed 2 dB there rather than the voice.

## Regenerating the stems

```bash
cd brand-film
python3 -I tools/make-audio.py
```

Every cue is a frame number in that script, so when the voice is recorded and a beat moves, change the frame there and re-run; the film's own timings live in `src/lib/timeline.ts` and the scenes.
